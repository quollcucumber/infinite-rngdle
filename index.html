Shows the badges and rarity for any number. Doesn't count towards your stats.

Every roll picks a uniformly random whole number from 0 to 1,000,000 using your browser's cryptographic RNG. Your number is scanned for patterns and each badge it matches is worth EP (entropy points).

A badge's EP comes from how many of the 1,000,001 possible numbers earn it: EP = 100 × 1,000,001 ÷ count (the same scale as RNGdle), so a badge half of all numbers get is worth 200 EP and a one-in-a-million badge is worth 100,000,100 EP. The badges are RNGdle's own 233, so any number scores the same here as there. Badges in the same family (e.g. Pair → Two Pair) only score their best member; the others are shown struck through.
